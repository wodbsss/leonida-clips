Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy covers "Leonida Clips Uploader", the tool Leonida Clips uses to upload its own videos to YouTube and Instagram. The tool is operated by the channel owner for the channel owner's accounts only.

What the tool accesses

  • YouTube Data API (scopes youtube.upload, youtube.readonly): to upload videos to the Leonida Clips channel and read that channel's own video list.
  • YouTube Analytics API (scope yt-analytics.readonly): to read view, watch-time and engagement numbers for the channel's own videos, so we can learn which clips work.
  • Instagram API: to publish Reels to the channel owner's own Instagram account and read their insights.

What we collect and store

We do not collect personal data from viewers or from any other user. The tool only stores, on the operator's own computer: the OAuth tokens for the operator's accounts, the IDs of videos it uploaded, and aggregate statistics for those videos. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept while the channel is active. Tokens can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions; revoking access stops the tool immediately, and local data is deleted on request.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
